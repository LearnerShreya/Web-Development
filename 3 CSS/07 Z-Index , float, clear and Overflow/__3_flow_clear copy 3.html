Summary

  • Floats: Use left or right to align elements within a container. Be cautious of layout issues with multiple floats.
  • Clear: clear: both; is best for ensuring elements sit below floated elements. clear: left or clear: right only affects left or right floats, respectively.
  • Clearfix: Use clearfix on containers with floated children to prevent them from collapsing.
  • Overflow: hidden: This can also be used on containers to expand them to fit floated children.
