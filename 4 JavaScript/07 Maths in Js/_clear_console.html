How to Clear Chrome Console

  1. Using the Console Button:
    • Open the Chrome Developer Tools (Ctrl + Shift + I or F12).
    • Navigate to the Console tab.
    • Click the clear console button, represented by a small circle with a line through it (🛇), located at the top-left corner of the console panel.
  2. Keyboard Shortcut:
    • Windows/Linux: Press Ctrl + L.
    • Mac: Press Cmd + K.
  3. Programmatically Clear the Console:
    • Type console.clear() in the console and press Enter.
    • This will clear the console output.
  4. Refresh the Page:
    • Simply refreshing the page (Ctrl + R or F5) will also clear the console, but this may reload resources and scripts.

Each method depends on your use case, but the keyboard shortcut and clear button are the quickest options for clearing clutter.
